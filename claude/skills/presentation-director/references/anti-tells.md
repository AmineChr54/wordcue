# What makes it look AI-generated

The only global style rule. Everything else is decided per project.

**Visual**
- Indigo-to-cyan or other decorative gradients, gradient headlines
- Blurred orbs and glows, dark navy with glow
- Translucent glass card grids, a dozen border radii
- Emoji as icons, sparkles or wands meaning AI
- The same kicker, heading and lead on every slide
- Neon tooltips, default SaaS KPI dashboards and progress bars for everything
- One fade-up applied to every element
- The default "AI editorial" look: cream, serif headline, one terracotta accent, unless the domain justifies each part
- Repeating an earlier project's look, signature move or structure, listed in `{{KIT}}\used-looks.md`, without the user asking for it

**Copy**
- Slogans, "nicht X, sondern Y", staccato triads
- Internal ticket codes on screen
- Figures with no label saying what they measure
