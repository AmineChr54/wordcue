# Recipe: pitch

**Status: not yet run through this kit.**

Win a room in a few minutes, for a hackathon or investors. Presented live, often with a demo inside, and with a video backup.

- **Stages:** brief, story rivals, art rivals, script, build, 2 review rounds. This is where rivals pay off the most.
- **Output:** one-file HTML for presenting and an MP4 of the same source as backup, in case the demo or the projector fails.
- **Structure:** a concrete person with a concrete problem, the moment it hurts, the demo doing the thing, why now and why you, one clear ask.
- **Timing:** the time limit is a hard constraint. Budget words at the speaker's real pace, starting from about 138 per minute, and rehearse against the presenter clock.
- **Demo:** staged in the engine, so it can't fail on stage. Say so if asked. Don't imply it's live when it isn't.
- **Techniques worth considering:** cursor timing based on distance, aiming off-centre on wide targets, spotlight focus and pace tracking.
