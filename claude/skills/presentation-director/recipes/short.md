# Recipe: short

**Status: used for one 19-second example film. Not yet used on a real short.**

Vertical, under about 60 seconds, for people scrolling. Start from `engine/examples/short` (900x1600 stage, 1080x1920 output).

- **Stages:** brief, a new look, script, build, one review round. Skip rivals unless the user wants options. Reuse a style only when the user says it's part of a series.
- **First 2 seconds decide everything:** open on the most surprising concrete thing, with big type. No logo, no greeting.
- **Pace:** a change every 1.5-3 seconds, 90-130 words for a minute, one idea only.
- **Layout:** keep text inside the middle 70% of the height, since platform UI covers the top and bottom. Type is large: nothing below about 44 px on the stage.
- **Captions:** burn in keywords, because many people watch muted.
- **End** on the point, or a loop back to the opening.
- **Publishing is the user's step.** Check voice-licence disclosure before posting, and never post client material.
