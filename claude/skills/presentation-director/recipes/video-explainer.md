# Recipe: video-explainer

**Status: tested end to end on one real film and the example films.**

Narrated film, no presenter. Landscape by default, MP4 first, with HTML from the same source.

- **Stages:** brief, story rivals if the angle is unclear, a new look (section 4 of the skill), script, voice, build, 2 review rounds, render both.
- **Length:** the brief sets it. To budget words, one film ran at about 155 words per minute including gaps, but measure again, because voice and script change this. Check the last line of `voice.py` before planning the edit.
- **Checks:** contact sheet, sync on key words, `--hash`, loudness.

Each film should find its own shape. Don't reuse the structure of an earlier project.
